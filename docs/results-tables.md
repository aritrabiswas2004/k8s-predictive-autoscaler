## The frontier test

Per repetition, R100, R80 and R60 form a piecewise-linear frontier of (pod-seconds, SLO-violation minutes). An arm's gap is its violation minutes minus the frontier at its cost: negative = fewer violations than over-provisioning buys for the same money.
